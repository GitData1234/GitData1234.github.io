Scoreboard Deployer - Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

Who uses it

Scoreboard Deployer is a private Google Apps Script tool used only by its owner, GitData1234, in his own Google account. It has no other users.

What Google data it accesses

With the permissions the owner grants in his own account, the tool:

  • reads and updates the content of the owner's own Apps Script projects;
  • reads the run history (executions) of those projects;
  • reads release files in a folder of the owner's Google Drive and renames the owner's own spreadsheets;
  • reads and writes its own spreadsheet;
  • sends emails to the owner.

How the data is used

Only to put the owner's scripts in place and to report to the owner whether they ran. Nothing is used for any other purpose.

Sharing and storage

No data is sold, shared with or sent to any third party. The tool stores nothing outside the owner's own Google account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
