Scoreboard Deployer

Scoreboard Deployer is a private Google Apps Script tool. It is used by one person, its owner, to look after his own Google Sheets dashboards.

What it does, inside the owner's own Google account:

  • reads release files the owner places in a folder of his own Google Drive;
  • puts the scripts from those files into the owner's own Apps Script projects, and renames the matching spreadsheets;
  • reads the run history of those projects to check that their scheduled jobs ran;
  • keeps its records in its own spreadsheet and sends status emails to the owner.

It is not offered to anyone else, has no other users, and does not collect, sell or share data.
